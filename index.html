Privacy Policy

Polymath.Ai · Last updated [DATE]

Polymath.Ai (“Polymath”, “we”, “us”) is an iOS app that explains homework problems from a photo. This policy describes what data the app handles and why. We have tried to keep it short and specific rather than long and vague.

The short version

  • You don’t create an account. We don’t know your name or email address.
  • Photos you scan are sent to be solved, then discarded. We don’t build a library of your homework.
  • Your saved solution history lives only on your device, not on our servers.
  • Free users can watch an optional ad to unlock more questions. Ads are served by Google AdMob.
  • Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Information we handle

Homework photos

When you scan a problem, the app crops and compresses the image on your device, then sends it to our servers, where it is processed by artificial intelligence services to read and solve the problem.

These images are processed to answer your question and are not intentionally retained afterwards. They are not used to train AI models, and they are not included in our normal operational logs.

Technical and usage data

Our servers keep records that contain no homework content — a request identifier, the image dimensions and file size, how long the request took, the estimated processing cost, and whether the request succeeded. We use these to monitor reliability, prevent abuse, and understand what the service costs to run.

Data stored only on your device

Your solution history, including a small thumbnail of each problem, is stored on your iPhone and is never uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes it. You can also remove individual entries from within the app.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photo library, or microphone. Polymath has no user accounts and no login.

Camera and photo access

The app asks for camera permission so you can photograph a problem. You can also choose an existing photo instead; that uses Apple’s photo picker, which gives the app only the single image you select and does not grant access to your photo library. You can change these permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.

We also use third-party cloud hosting and artificial intelligence providers to process scanned problems and return an answer. These providers act on our instructions and receive only what is needed to answer your question.

We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising

Polymath is free to use, and ads are what cover the cost of running the servers and the AI that solves each problem. New users receive a number of free questions; after those are used, you can choose to watch a short video ad to unlock more. Ads are never forced on you mid-problem. Users who subscribe to Polymath Pro see no ads at all.

Your remaining question balance is stored on your device.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use a device identifier to limit how often you see the same ad and to measure ad performance.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before serving personalised ads, and you can change that choice at any time from the Privacy Options item in the app’s Profile screen.

On iOS you also control app tracking through Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Subscriptions

Polymath Pro is billed through your Apple Account. Apple handles the payment and tells our app only whether a subscription is currently active. We never receive or store your card details. You can manage or cancel a subscription in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Children’s privacy

Polymath is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Because Polymath has no accounts and does not keep your homework, we usually hold no personal information tied to you that we could look up, export, or delete on request.

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or a US state with its own privacy law, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, and to object to certain processing. To exercise them, contact us at the address below. To remove everything the app has stored on your device, delete the app.

Data retention

Homework images are kept only as long as it takes to answer your question. Technical logs, which contain no homework content, are retained for a limited period for reliability and cost monitoring.

Security

Data travels to our servers over an encrypted connection, and requests are verified as coming from a genuine copy of the app. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
